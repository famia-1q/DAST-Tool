Executive Summary

Acme Corp ("Acme Corp" herein) contracted iSeeWaves (Private) Limited to perform a security assessment to identify security weaknesses, determine the impact, document all findings in a clear and repeatable manner, and provide remediation recommendations.

Approach

iSeeWaves performed testing under a "black box" approach on {{ scan_date }} without credentials or any advance knowledge of Acme Corp's environment. Testing was performed from a non-evasive standpoint with the goal of uncovering as many misconfigurations and vulnerabilities as possible.

Scope

The scope of this assessment was the Acme Corp target artifact and associated endpoints.

Remediation Summary

As a result of this assessment there are several opportunities for Acme Corp to strengthen its security posture. Remediation efforts are prioritized below.

Short Term

  • Review all High and Medium severity findings from engine outputs.
  • Address critical misconfigurations immediately.

Medium Term

  • Integrate automated scanning into CI/CD pipeline.
  • Configure severity-based gating.

Long Term

  • Perform ongoing internal vulnerability assessments.
  • Establish continuous compliance monitoring.
