Remediation Summary

As a result of this assessment there are several opportunities for {{ client_name }} to strengthen its security posture. Remediation efforts are prioritized below.

Short Term

  • Review all High and Medium severity findings from engine outputs.
  • Address critical misconfigurations immediately.

Medium Term

  • Integrate automated scanning into CI/CD pipeline.
  • Configure severity-based gating to prevent vulnerable code deployment.

Long Term

  • Perform ongoing internal vulnerability assessments.
  • Establish continuous compliance monitoring against OWASP ASVS/MASVS.
